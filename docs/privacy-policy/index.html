Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CareerLeap ("CareerLeap", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the CareerLeap mobile application and its resume-analysis service. It applies to the CareerLeap app published by the individual developer responsible for this service.

In short: CareerLeap uses your email address to authenticate your account, processes the resume and optional job description you submit to generate a report, and stores your scan-credit balance and related ledger entries. Resume and job-description content is not saved to a CareerLeap scan-history database.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, Firebase user identifier, email-verification status and authentication information needed to create and secure your account. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; CareerLeap's application database does not store your password.
  • Resume and job-description content: the PDF or DOCX resume you choose to upload, its file name and document metadata, and any optional job description you enter. This content is sent to our backend to parse and analyse it and produce your report.
  • Credit and transaction information: your Firebase user identifier, scan-credit balance, reserved-credit count, credit transaction amounts and types, transaction references, idempotency keys and timestamps. These records are stored in Neon PostgreSQL to operate the scan-credit system. The credit database does not need your resume contents.
  • Technical and service information: information that may be generated when the app and service communicate, such as IP address, request time, device/operating-system details, app version and diagnostic or security logs. Our hosting and authentication providers may process some of this information to provide, secure and troubleshoot their services.
  • Messages to support: if you email us, we receive the email address and the contents and attachments you choose to send.

2. How resume analysis works

When you submit a resume, the app sends the selected file and, if supplied, your job description to the CareerLeap backend hosted by Render. The backend processes the submitted content to extract information and calculate resume-quality and job-match feedback. The current analyzer uses deterministic, rule-based analysis; it does not send resume content to a generative-AI or large-language-model provider.

Resume and job-description content is processed for the request and is not written to CareerLeap's scan-history database or Neon credit ledger. The app does not currently provide a saved scan-history feature. The service and its infrastructure providers may handle transient technical data and request logs as described in this policy.

3. Why we use information

  • To create and authenticate accounts and verify email addresses.
  • To process resumes and optional job descriptions and return analysis reports.
  • To maintain scan-credit balances, prevent duplicate scan charges and record credit transactions.
  • To protect the app, detect misuse, troubleshoot errors and maintain service reliability.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important service information.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

4. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share or make information available only as needed to operate CareerLeap, protect the service, or meet legal obligations. Current service providers include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: account creation, email verification and sign-in. Google processes account and authentication data under its own terms and privacy documentation.
  • Render: hosting and operation of the CareerLeap backend. Resume files and optional job descriptions are transmitted to this backend for analysis.
  • Neon: managed PostgreSQL database hosting for scan-credit wallets and transaction records.
  • Google Play: app distribution and related store services. Google Play handles information under Google's own privacy terms.

These providers may process information on systems located in countries other than your own and may collect technical information necessary to provide their services. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy terms and contractual arrangements with us.

5. Retention

Resume and job-description content is processed for the analysis request and is not retained in a CareerLeap scan-history database. Account information is retained by Firebase while the account is active. Credit balances and transaction records are retained as needed to operate the credit ledger, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and maintain appropriate business records. Support correspondence and technical logs may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

Where information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or make it no longer identifiable, subject to legal, security and operational requirements and the retention practices of our service providers.

6. Security

We use service providers and reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including authenticated access to account-specific services and encrypted connections where supported. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

You can choose whether to provide a resume or optional job description. You can stop using the app and sign out at any time. To ask a question, request access to or correction of information, or request deletion of your CareerLeap account and associated data, contact us at careerleap0@gmail.com from your registered email address. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention or legitimate operational purposes.

8. Children's privacy

CareerLeap is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as CareerLeap's features, data practices or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date. Please review it periodically.
